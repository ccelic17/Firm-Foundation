1. What this is

Firm Foundation is a daily discipline app for Christian men, covering physical, mental and spiritual practice. It is a personal accountability tool. It is not a church, a counselling service, a medical device, or a crisis service.

By using it you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use it.

You must be 16 or older.

2. What you get, free and paid

Free, with no time limit: check-ins, streaks, domain scores, priorities, daily disciplines, journal, prayer lists, Bible reader, verse of the day, scripture library, Sabbath Mode, wearable sync, and the shareable card. Plus the daily personalised opening and Governor message.

New users get 7 days of full access. When those 7 days end nothing is charged and nothing auto-renews — the app simply returns to the free allowances above. You are never billed unless you actively choose to subscribe.

Paid removes those limits: $9.99 per month or $79 per year.

3. Billing, renewal and cancellation

  • Payment is processed by Stripe. We never see your card details.
  • Prices are in US dollars and shown before you pay. There are no hidden fees. Tax may be added based on your location.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically — monthly plans monthly, annual plans annually — at the same price, until you cancel.
  • Cancel any time, and it takes no more effort than subscribing did. Use the "Manage subscription" link in your Stripe receipt email, or email support@thefirmfoundation.app and we will cancel it for you. We will not make you call, sit through retention offers, or navigate obstacles.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. Access continues to the end of the period you already paid for, then returns to the free tier. Your data is untouched.
  • Refunds: if you are unhappy, email us within 14 days of a charge and we will refund it, no argument. After 14 days, refunds are at our discretion — but ask, especially if you were charged for a renewal you did not intend.
  • If we change prices, existing subscribers get notice before the change takes effect and can cancel first.

4. AI, and its limits — read this

The app uses Anthropic's Claude for the opening greeting, Governor message, prayer prompts, scripture search, 90-day plans, and The Council's five mentors.

Everything the AI produces is machine-generated. It is not written or reviewed by a pastor, a counsellor, a doctor, or any human before you see it. It can be wrong, and it can misquote or invent scripture despite instructions not to. Check every scripture reference against your Bible.

It is not professional advice. Nothing the AI says is medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. The Governor message comments on wearable data and is not a medical judgement about your body. Do not use this app to make health decisions. Talk to your doctor.

It is not a crisis service. The AI is instructed to respond to self-harm, suicidal thinking or crisis by pointing you to real human help. That instruction can fail. If you are in crisis, contact people, not this app: in the US call or text 988; internationally, findahelpline.com; if anyone is in immediate danger, emergency services.

It is not spiritual authority. The Council's mentors are AI personas. They are not ordained, they do not know you, and they are no substitute for your pastor, your church, or Scripture itself.
